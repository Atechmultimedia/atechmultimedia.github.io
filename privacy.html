Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Last updated: May 2026

Atech Multimedia Entertainment ("Atech", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Name, email address, phone number, and project details submitted through our contact or quote forms
  • Order information submitted through our Order Center
  • Messages sent to us via WhatsApp or email

Information collected automatically

  • General usage data (pages visited, time on site) if analytics tools are enabled
  • Browser type and device type (used to improve site performance)
  • IP address (logged by hosting servers for security purposes)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and quote requests
  • Process orders and communicate about project delivery
  • Send invoices and payment confirmations
  • Improve our website and services
  • Send updates about our services if you have opted in

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Contact Form & Formspree

Our contact and quote forms are processed by Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form service. When you submit a form, your data is sent to Formspree's servers and then forwarded to our email. Formspree's own privacy policy applies to how they handle this data.

4. WhatsApp Communications

If you contact us via WhatsApp, your messages are subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy. We use WhatsApp solely to respond to customer enquiries and share order updates.

5. AI Chat (Ama)

Our website includes an AI chat assistant ("Ama") powered by Anthropic's Claude API. When you use the chat:

  • Your messages are sent to our secure proxy server and then to Anthropic's API
  • Conversation history is stored in your browser session only and is not permanently retained by us
  • Anthropic's Privacy Policy applies to API data handling

6. Cookies

We use a minimal cookie to remember your cookie consent preference. If analytics are enabled, analytics cookies may also be set. You can decline cookies using the banner shown on your first visit, and you can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. Our website is served over HTTPS. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain contact and order information for as long as necessary to fulfil your request and maintain business records (typically up to 3 years). You may request deletion of your data at any time.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@atechmultimedia.com or via WhatsApp: +233 24 326 6013.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (social media, client websites, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Your continued use of our website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
